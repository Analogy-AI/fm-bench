# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in FM-Bench to be a straightforward, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being direct about technical disagreement without making it personal.
- Giving and accepting corrections gracefully — especially about measurements.
- Reporting a problem you found in someone else's work as a finding, not as a
  judgment of them.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

One benchmark-specific note: **claiming a score you did not measure, or
concealing a known information leak or exploit in order to preserve a result,
is a conduct issue here and not merely a technical one.** The value of this
project rests entirely on its numbers being honest.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **support@analogyai.org**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or ban
participants for behavior they judge inappropriate, and will explain the
decision when doing so.
